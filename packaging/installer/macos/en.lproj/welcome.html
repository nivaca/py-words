py-words @VERSION@ is a Latin dictionary and parser: it analyses Latin words and gives their meanings, and finds Latin words for English or Spanish ones.

This installer puts in your Applications folder:

  • py-words.app, the dictionary in a window, with a Python of its own (nothing else is needed);
  • optionally, py-words (CLI), py-words (TUI) and py-words (Web), which open the command line, the full-screen terminal version and the web page from Terminal;
  • optionally, the py-words command in /usr/local/bin, for Terminal.

“Customize”, on the page “Installation Type”, chooses among them. It replaces any earlier version. Your settings and history are kept.
